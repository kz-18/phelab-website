Research focus

Our team studies how religious beliefs and community values shape attitudes towards vaccines. We aim to learn directly from community members about how their backgrounds and faith traditions influence their views.

Aim 1

To investigate religious-based attitudes towards three types of vaccines among Christian and Jewish community leaders and pre-health students in the Phoenix metropolitan area

Aim 2

To begin curriculum development targeted for pre-health students that explores religion-based attitudes towards vaccine hesitancy

Aim 3

To investigate the generalizability of our findings by surveying a nationally representative sample of healthcare workers in the US
