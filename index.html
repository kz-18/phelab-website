Research focus

Our team studies how religious beliefs and community values shape attitudes towards vaccines. We aim to learn directly from community members about how their backgrounds and faith traditions influence their views.

Vaccine hesitancy

Understanding why communities trust or distrust vaccination guidance, and how messaging can close that gap without coercion.

Social determinants of health

Mapping how housing, income, and access to care compound to produce unequal health outcomes across populations.

Ethics of public health policy

Examining the tradeoffs policymakers face between individual liberty and collective wellbeing during health interventions.
